Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 2026-09-28

Browser-only calculations

CalcKaro calculators are implemented as client-side JavaScript. Inputs you enter into calculator fields are processed in your browser for the calculation result.

Information we collect

  • Calculator inputs: Not collected by default in this repository setup.
  • Accounts: No user account or login is required to use calculators.
  • Contact messages: If you use the contact form/mailto flow, your email client sends the details you choose to share.

Ads and analytics status

This repository does not include active AdSense or analytics IDs by default. Integration placeholders may be present for optional setup by the site owner after approval/consent configuration.

Third-party services

If the site owner later enables third-party ads or analytics, those services may process data under their own privacy policies. The owner must update this page when such integrations are activated.

Cookies

No first-party tracking cookies are intentionally set by this repository's default calculator code.

Changes to this policy

Policy updates are published on this page with the revised review date.
