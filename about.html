About CalcKaro

CalcKaro is a free static website with practical financial calculators for Indian users. The currently published tools are SIP, income tax comparison, and home loan EMI.

How the site works

Calculations run in your browser using JavaScript on each page. Inputs are not sent to a backend by default. Results are estimates and should be verified with official sources and your financial/tax advisor when needed.

Maintainer

This repository is maintained by Mayank Khadiya. No claims are made here about professional credentials, certifications, or regulated advisory services.

Data freshness and review policy

Tax slabs, deductions, and lender policies can change. Any year-specific figures on calculator pages should be treated as page assumptions until re-verified. Before updating figures, check official government notifications and lender websites.

  • Income tax assumptions: verify on the Income Tax Department/CBDT websites before edits.
  • Home loan rate examples: verify directly from lender websites before edits.
  • Review dates are shown on each calculator page and legal page.

Monetization and analytics status

This repository includes only placeholders for optional ads and analytics integrations. No active publisher ID or analytics ID is shipped in source by default.
